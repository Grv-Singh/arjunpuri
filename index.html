gavel Arjunpuri 1st Link Road Community Charter

Consensus rules agreed upon to maintain hygiene, peace, and smooth vehicle movement in our narrow link road.

volume_off 1. Quiet Hours & Construction Timing

Heavy noise construction, stone cutting, drilling, and hammer work is strictly permitted only between 06:00 AM and 10:00 PM. Night work is prohibited.

construction 2. Construction Malba & Dust Control

Any demolition debris (malba) must be lifted within 7 days. Green safety netting and water sprinkling must be used to prevent dust from blowing into neighboring homes.

directions_car 3. Clear Passage & Lane Parking Ethics

Vehicles must be parked strictly against designated plot walls. The center lane must remain free for emergency vehicles, water tankers, and garbage vans.

delete_forever 4. Waste & Water Drainage

All wastewater from car washing must flow directly into naali chambers, never onto the public street to prevent mosquito breeding and slippery mud.
