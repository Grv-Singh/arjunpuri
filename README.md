# Arjunpuri 1st — Community & Resident Portal

Digital Registry & Civic Management Portal for **Arjunpuri 1st Link Road, Imli Phatak, Tonk Phatak, Jaipur — 302015**.

## Features
- **All 31 Plots & Resident Directory**: Comprehensive mapping of plot numbers, resident names, contact details, police verification records, and Aadhaar verification status.
- **Instant Vehicle-to-Plot Lookup**: Real-time plate number search (e.g. `8141`, `9849`) to resolve parking blockages directly without group arguments.
- **Civic Authorities Directory**: 1-click call and WhatsApp to Ward Parshad Prem Chand, Nagar Nigam Sanitation Officers, BJP Karyakarta, local Electrician, and Police beat.
- **Civic Action Tracker**: Live status of JVVNL underground cabling petitions, BSNL pole removal, sewerage line jetting, and gate NOCs.
- **Colony Charter & By-Laws**: Clear consensus rules on construction dust mitigation, quiet hours (10 PM – 6 AM), and parking ethics.

## Design
- Built with vanilla HTML, modern CSS, and lightweight JavaScript.
- Iconography exclusively powered by **Android Material Symbols Rounded**.
- Mobile-first responsive glassmorphism interface.
